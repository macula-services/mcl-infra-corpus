## One-page summary

In the cloud, cost stops being a procurement line and becomes an
engineering property — **FinOps** makes it visible (Inform), reduces it
(Optimize), and keeps it that way (Operate). **Multi-cloud** is the
deliberate use of several providers; its design principles (domain
boundaries, API-first, abstraction at the seams) matter more than any
single provider's API.
