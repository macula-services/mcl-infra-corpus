## One-page summary

**Observability** is the ability to understand a system — whether
something is wrong, and why. The three pillars: **metrics** (numbers
over time), **logs** (discrete records), **traces** (a request's path
across services). Beyond them: profiling data at the resource level and
events at the platform level. Different people ask different questions
of the same data — the design of an observability platform starts from
those personas.
